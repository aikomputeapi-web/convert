WHY THIS EXISTS

Built different, on purpose.

01

Runs entirely offline

Every conversion happens locally via WebAssembly. Nothing you drop here is ever sent anywhere.

02

Format-agnostic

Convert between wildly different mediums — images to audio, documents to 3D models, and everything between.

03

Zero setup

No accounts, no installs, no waiting rooms. Drop a file, pick a format, and you're already done.

04

Open source

Fully auditable and GPL-licensed, built in the open by a community of contributors.

05

No file limits

There's no server quota, no daily cap and no size ceiling — your own device is the only limit.

06

Built for tinkerers

A pluggable handler architecture makes it straightforward to add support for new formats.

THREE STEPS

About as simple as it gets.

  1. 01

    Drop your file

  2. 02

    Download instantly
